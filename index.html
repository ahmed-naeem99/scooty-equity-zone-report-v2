What is a vehicle-hour?

One scooter sitting or being available in an area for one hour equals 1 vehicle-hour. Ten scooters there for ten hours equals 100 vehicle-hours.

Positive gap

Demand is higher than scooter time. Example: +40 hours/day means the zone could probably use about 40 more scooter-hours per day.

Negative gap

Scooter time is higher than demand. Example: -40 hours/day means this zone may have about 40 extra scooter-hours per day.
